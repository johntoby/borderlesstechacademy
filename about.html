About BTA

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't — yet.

Borderless Tech Academy exists to close that gap: live, practical tech training that ends in a real internship and a global paycheck, not just a certificate.

Our vision

Talent shouldn't need a passport.

We make Africans globally employable through practical, borderless tech education — the kind that bridges the classroom and a real job from week one.

Our mission

10,000 Africans, earning globally, by 2030.

We equip people with in-demand tech skills and mentorship from industry experts, so they can earn in dollars from anywhere in the world.

Why BTA

Theory gets you started. A real project gets you hired.

Africa has plenty of tech training institutes. What sets BTA apart is the real-world experience built into every program — every learner applies what they've learned to a live project with StockSmartAI, our internship partner. From there, two paths open up.

Build it into a startup

Keep going on your internship project after the cohort ends, and turn it into something you own.

Walk into interviews ready

Talk through a real product you helped ship — not a tutorial clone everyone else has too.

Our manifesto

We don't just teach technology. We teach freedom.

We are dreamers. Builders. Believers in a new Africa — one where talent, not geography, defines destiny.

We believe hope is not a privilege. It's a skill — one that can be learned, built, and deployed, just like DevOps, Cloud, or Code.

We see a generation of Africans tired of waiting for opportunities to arrive. So we teach them to build their own.

With a laptop, an internet connection, and the right mindset, anyone can rise — from Lagos to London, Nairobi to New York, Accra to Amsterdam.

Freedom from limits. Freedom from borders. Freedom to dream, build, and belong to a global digital tribe.
